====================================================================================================

(base 0x456210, 8 records x 0x374 stride, ends 0x457db0) and were
deliberately NOT renamed/typed in pass 5c (docs/11-issues.md section 16,
'protected region': do NOT re-type - they are interior aliases of the
typed record array). Use this table to resolve a DAT_* you meet in
decompilation to its logical g_playerRecords[i].<field>.

(= g_apPlayers @0x456360, Player[8]). Field names below use the
record-relative offset; aliases between the two views are marked.
